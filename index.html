Our story

Chai, vada pav & green fields

Chai वडा by Vaishu is an open-air café sitting right beside lush green fields — a place to slow down with a hot kulhad of chai and a spicy vada pav.

A straw-roof chai hut, festive bunting, string lights and hills on the horizon. It's where friends gather in the evening, families unwind on weekends, and travellers stop for “just one cutting”.
